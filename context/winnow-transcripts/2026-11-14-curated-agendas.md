## Q&A

- **How should the work be split?** Three tickets: find, clone, and modify
  (swap at the session level).
- **Who are the four, and what is the feature called?** Tinker, Tailor,
  Soldier and Spy — four new seeded attendees who are not speakers. The
  feature is "Curated agendas".
- **What happens when everyone clones the same agenda?** Each curator has a
  cap.
- **How big is the cap?** Attendees divided by curators — 25 for 100 attendees
  and 4 curators. Taken as: rounded up, curators not counted as attendees, one
  clone per attendee, full sessions still give a waitlist place, and a full
  curator can still be viewed.
- **What if you already have sessions when you clone?** You can't: cloning
  always starts from an empty agenda, and changing individual sessions comes
  after.
- **Show curator picks on session cards?** Not now.
- **Where do these live?** Swapping builds My Agenda, with Other options and
  Swap per session; finding is a new Curated agendas page linked from the
  menu and Home.
